Tips
  • Length beats complexity: 16 random words beat "P@ssw0rd1".
  • Reuse is the real enemy — use a password manager and unique credentials.
  • Turn on 2FA. A breach-resistant password plus phishing-resistant MFA is the modern baseline.
  • Avoid personal info (dates, names, team names) and keyboard patterns.
